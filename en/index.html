FR

Netersoft builds free software.

Apps, online services, libraries or content: whatever their form, they follow the same principles.

Principles

  1. 01

    Freedom

    Anyone can use, study, modify and redistribute what we publish. Apps are under the GNU GPL v3, online services under the GNU AGPL v3, libraries under MIT or Apache 2.0, and content under CC BY-SA 4.0.

  2. 02

    Transparency

    Source code is public, so what a piece of software does can be checked rather than merely claimed.

  3. 03

    No cost

    Access to the software depends neither on payment nor on exposure to advertising.

  4. 04

    Restraint

    Software only processes the data it needs to work. Nothing is collected for any other purpose, shared or sold.
