Privacy policy

Effective October 24, 2026.

Siamath is a free app for learning maths by understanding why it works, published by Netersoft. It works entirely offline: this policy explains what it does with your data, which is nothing outside your device.

No data collected

Siamath does not collect, send or sell any personal data. The app does not ask for Internet access, has no user account, contains no artificial intelligence and no analytics, advertising or crash reporting tools, and talks to no server, Netersoft's included.

Data stored on your device

  • Your progress: where you are in each notion, your answers (including your first attempt at the starting problem and the answers the app could not analyse), the hints and solutions you opened, and the status of each notion.
  • Your explanations: the texts you write to explain a notion in your own words. They are only used to show them back to you and are read by no one else.
  • Your preferences (theme, text size, language, first launch) are stored unencrypted: they contain no learning data.

Your progress and explanations are encrypted with AES-256, under a key kept in the system's secure storage (Android Keystore, iOS Keychain). On Android, they are excluded from backups and from transfers to a new device: they exist only on your device. In the web version, they stay in your browser's storage.

"Erase everything", in the settings, permanently deletes this data, as does uninstalling the app or, on the web, clearing the site's data.

Recommending the app

"Recommend the app" opens your device's sharing, text messages or e-mail with a message you can edit. You choose who to send it to; Siamath sends nothing else.

Children

Siamath is also meant for underage pupils. Since the app collects no data, it collects no children's data either, and it contains no advertising and no purchases.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will ship with the app update, along with its effective date.

Contact

For any question about this policy or the app, write to support.netersoft@gmail.com.
